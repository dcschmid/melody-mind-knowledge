# Design system: MelodyMind Reviews

## Creative North Star

The record under one reading light. An ink-blue room, warm type, one teal signal,
and a review whose structure remains visible without becoming a dashboard.

## Layout

Article openings pair a rights-safe typographic album mark with the review thesis.
Desktop articles use a narrow Review Map rail beside a 68–72 character reading column.
Mobile keeps the same order and places the map before the text. Lists use dividers,
not repeated cards.

## Typography and color

Atkinson Hyperlegible carries album titles, editorial headings, body text,
metadata, navigation, and controls. Warm off-white is the reading color.
Teal marks format, active map position, and key links; it is not decoration.
Editorial identity comes from type scale, spacing, and asymmetry—not a second
face or color.

## Motion and accessibility

Only navigation and current Review Map state change. The complete article, maps,
sources, and links work without JavaScript. Focus is visible, targets are at least
44px, and Forced Colors preserves boundaries and state.
